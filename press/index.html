Description

Online co-op physics comedy for 1–4 friends. Go ice fishing on a floe that drifts, tilts and cracks.

After one banya too many, a crew of ice fishermen sets off north on a floe after the legendary Tsar-Pike — a fish nobody has landed in a hundred years. The floe is melting. The current only goes one way.

Up the Creek! is an online co-op physics comedy for 1–4 players. Everything is done in first person with your own two hands: drill, cast, haul, grab, throw, slap.

Features

  • Online co-op for 2–4 friends; playable solo
  • Physics hands: anything you hold can be dropped, thrown or handed to a friend
  • Keep the floe level: everyone on one edge means everyone in the water
  • Rescue or sabotage: pull a friend out of the water, or help them in
  • A saved season: five bays, a shared pot, upgrades, and the Tsar-Pike at the far end
  • Evenings in the village: the "Bukhanka" van, the drying racks, the workshop, the banya
